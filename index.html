About

PhD Student in Industrial Engineering at Northeastern University

My name is Negar (Niki) Sadeghi. I am a PhD student in Industrial Engineering at Northeastern University, working at the intersection of generative AI, optimization, simulation, and data-driven decision support.

My research focuses on healthcare and personalized medicine supply chains, where I develop optimization and simulation models to support complex, time-sensitive operational decisions.

I also work with large language models, prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, AI agents, and AI-assisted analytics, with an interest in translating analytical results into practical decision-support tools.
